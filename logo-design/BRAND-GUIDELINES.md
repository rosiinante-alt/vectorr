# Vectroy Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Vectroy** is an AI-powered vector conversion platform that transforms raster images (PNG, JPG, WEBP) into production-ready vector graphics (SVG, DXF, EPS, PDF, G-code, DST, AI). Our brand identity reflects precision, intelligence, and transformation—bridging the gap between artistic intent and technical execution.

---

## Logo System

### Primary Mark: Diamond/Kite V-Split

The **Diamond/Kite V-Split** is Vectroy's primary brand mark. It represents:
- **Left Diamond (Cyan→Blue)**: Input/raster (photography, images)
- **Right Diamond (Purple→Magenta)**: Output/vector (precision, computational)
- **Center Line**: The transformation pathway between input and output

**Geometry**: 200×200 viewBox, geometric diamonds meeting at a center point.

#### Primary Logo
- **File**: `concept-c-diamond-kite-v2.svg` (production, no effects)
- **Use**: Primary brand mark for all applications
- **Minimum Size**: 32px (favicon use)
- **Colors**: Dual gradients (see Color Palette below)

#### Presentation Version
- **File**: `concept-c-diamond-kite-presentation.svg`
- **Use**: Presentations, marketing materials, social media
- **Features**: Glow effects for visual impact
- **Background**: Dark (#0F0F1A)

#### Light Variant
- **File**: `concept-c-diamond-kite-light.svg`
- **Use**: Light backgrounds, white/grey surfaces
- **Colors**: White to light grey gradients

## Lockup Specifications

### Horizontal Lockup
```
[Logo Mark (50×50px)] [30px gap] [Vectroy Wordmark + Tagline]
```
- **Minimum width**: 280px
- **Height**: 80px
- **Alignment**: Vertical center of mark with baseline of wordmark
- **Use**: Websites, marketing materials, print

### Stacked Lockup
```
[Logo Mark (60×60px)]
[10px gap]
[Vectroy Wordmark]

```
- **Width**: 120px minimum

- **Alignment**: Center
- **Use**: Square social avatars, app icon contexts, tight spaces

### Icon-Only (No Wordmark)
- **File**: `concept-c-diamond-kite-v2.svg` or variants
- **Use**: When space is extremely limited, favicon use, single-color contexts
- **Minimum**: 32×32px (avoid below this size)

## Typography

### Wordmark
- **Typeface**: Modern sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, or system sans-serif)
- **Weight**: 700 (Bold)
- **Size**: Scales with context; typically 48–72px for digital
- **Letter Spacing**: -1px to -2px (tight, modern feel)
- **Color**: White (#FFFFFF) on dark, dark (#333333) on light
- **Case**: Title case "Vectroy" (always capitalized)

### Tagline
- **Text**: "AI Vector Conversion"
- **Typeface**: Same as wordmark
- **Weight**: 400 (Regular)
- **Size**: Typically 30–40% of wordmark size
- **Letter Spacing**: 0.5px to 1px (relaxed)
- **Color**: Primary accent (Cyan #00B8D4) or muted text (#888888)

### Descriptor (Optional)
- **Text**: "Raster to Vector. Perfected." or "Transform Images into Vectors"
- **Weight**: 400 (Regular)
- **Size**: 18–24px (digital)
- **Color**: Muted text (#AAAAAA)

## Clear Space & Minimum Size

### Clear Space (Internal Padding)
- **Minimum**: 10% of logo height on all sides
- **Example**: 200×200px logo → 20px minimum clear space on all sides
- **Exception**: Favicon/icon-only use can reduce to 5px minimum

### Minimum Size
- **Digital Display**: 32×32px (web, app icons)
- **Print**: 0.5 inches × 0.5 inches (12.7mm)
- **Mobile App**: 40×40px (safe visual minimum)
- **Below 32px**: Logo remains recognizable but accent line becomes subtle

### Maximum Size
- **No hard limit**: Logo scales infinitely (vector format)
- **Recommendation**: Do not exceed 50% of container width for horizontal lockups

## Do's & Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Use the logo on the provided dark background (#0F0F1A) by default
- Maintain the 45° and 135° gradient directions for visual consistency
- Scale proportionally (maintain aspect ratio)
- Apply glow effects only in presentation contexts (not production)
- Use the light variant on light/white backgrounds
- Include minimum clear space around the logo
- Ensure sufficient contrast with background colors
- Use the diamond/kite V-split as the primary mark

### ❌ Don't
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Change gradient directions or color stops
- Add drop shadows or other non-approved effects to production versions
- Use the logo on low-contrast backgrounds without testing
- Apply effects to the favicon or icon variants
- Change the aspect ratio or proportions
- Use incomplete or partial versions of the logo
- Combine with other competing brand marks
- Stretch or distort the geometric shapes
- Apply motion or animation to the production SVG

## Export Formats

All files are provided as **SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)** for maximum flexibility. Additional formats can be generated as needed:

- **PNG/JPG**: Rasterize SVGs at desired resolution (1×, 2×, 3× for screens)
- **PDF**: For print applications
- **AI/EPS**: For design software workflows
- **ICO**: For favicon (convert 32px SVG)
- **WEBP**: For modern web optimization

### Recommended Export Settings
- **PNG**: 2× resolution (e.g., 64×64px from 32px base)
- **Favicon**: ICO format, 32×32px
- **App Store**: Provide 1×, 2×, 3× variants
- **Print**: 300 DPI minimum, CMYK color mode

## Accessibility

### Color Contrast
- **Cyan→Blue gradient**: WCAG AA compliant on #0F0F1A (≥4.5:1)
- **Purple→Magenta gradient**: WCAG AA compliant on #0F0F1A (≥4.5:1)
- **Light variant**: WCAG AA on white/light backgrounds

### Scalability
- Logo remains visually recognizable from 32px (web) to 4 inches (print)
- Geometric shapes maintain clarity at all sizes
- Accent line subtle but present at sizes ≥64px

### Color Independence
- Do not rely solely on color to convey meaning
- Diamond shape and geometric clarity provide shape-based recognition
- Works in grayscale contexts (though not recommended)

### Screen Readers
- SVG alt text: "Vectroy logo—AI vector conversion platform"
- Logo link alt: "Vectroy home" or "Return to home"
