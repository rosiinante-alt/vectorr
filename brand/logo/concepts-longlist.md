# Vectroy — concepts longlist

Spread across mark types. Scored 1–5 on idea clarity, distinction, simplicity, relevance, small-size strength.

| # | Concept (one sentence) | Type | C | D | S | R | 16px | Σ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **True Trace** — a V whose left arm is a stepped 4-px raster edge and whose right arm is one clean vector edge, the two meeting at a single point. | letterform | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 23 |
| 2 | **The Cut** — a V drawn as two blades of a knife-cut: the vector is the cut line, the raster is the scrap left behind (small detached sliver). | abstract | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 19 |
| 3 | **Node V** — the V is two straight paths, with a single square node handle sitting exactly at the vertex: the control point that keeps it true. | letterform | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 21 |
| 4 | **Spool V** — a V drawn as a thread path leaving an embroidery spool, tying the DST/embroidery output into the mark. | pictorial | 3 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 15 |
| 5 | **Simplified** — a dense hatch block reduces, left to right, to three clean strokes: "curves, not noise". | abstract | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 17 |
| 6 | **Trace-through** — a single stroke enters from outside the tile and exits as a perfect geometric V, "the image became the vector". | abstract | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 17 |
| 7 | **Monogram VT** — V and t fused at the vertex, the t crossbar extending the V's right arm into a horizontal. | monogram | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 13 |
| 8 | **Prism V** — the V is the negative space between two overlapping traced shapes (the cut between before/after). | monogram | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 17 |
| 9 | **Blade · Mat** — a V-shaped laser beam cutting a slowly-stepped edge in a solid field. | pictorial | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 14 |
| 10 | **Arc V** — V formed by two true circular arcs tangent at the vertex: "a curve told exactly". | letterform | 3 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 16 |
| 11 | **True/Guess** — the V's right arm continues into an anchor dot (the point the trace snapped to); left arm stays stepped. Variant of 1, rejected as clutter. | letterform | 4 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 17 |
| 12 | **Wide V** — the name's V set as an optical wedge that also reads as an open path (start and end nodes). | letterform | 2 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 14 |

**The three built:** **1 True Trace**, **3 Node V**, **5 Simplified** — three genuinely different mark types
(letterform-with-craft, geometric letterform, abstract), all fixing the same strategy: *crisp, true, precise*.

Rejected early: 4 and 9 (craft pictorial — literal product drawing), 7 (monogram mushy at 16 px),
10 (too close to stock geometric V marks), 12 (no ownable twist).
